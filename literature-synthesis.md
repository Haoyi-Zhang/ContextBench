# Literature positioning for context transport

## Reference accounting and verification scope

The manuscript cites 62 scholarly records, classified as 61 peer-reviewed
publications and the Semantic Trap preprint. The comparison matrix has 72 unique
works: 24 recorded structural readings and 48 claim-specific verifications.
These are different reading depths. A stored structural-reading status does not
mean every implementation or appendix was rederived in the current check.
Verification dates and primary records are explicit in the inventory. The
reproduction audit performs no live registry query; it checks cross-file closure,
not truth by repetition. CodeXGLUE is recorded in the dedicated NeurIPS Datasets
and Benchmarks proceedings, volume 1; no unsupported global pagination is used.
EIP-1470 remains only an external terminology resource, not a manuscript reference.

The matrix retains 12 same-track, five distinguished/high-visibility, five
adjacent-top-venue, and six foundational roles. A paper can serve multiple roles
without becoming multiple unique works. Venue-role counts are calibration aids,
not evidence of novelty or acceptance quality.

## Closest methodological alternatives

Relational Logic with Framing and Hypotheses supplies compositional relational
reasoning about effects and unchanged state. Cartesian Hoare Logic treats richer
k-safety properties without materializing their Cartesian execution product.
Self-composition, product programs, Benton's relational logic, hyperproperties,
and language-based information-flow security supply the broader multi-run and
noninterference foundations. Abstract interpretation supplies the type/interval
reasoning used for totality. The present result does not introduce these methods,
a new general logic, or a new general abstract domain.

The narrower object is a consumer-checked transport assertion for a supplied
vulnerable/patched family. It combines exact repair membership, fixed security
and abort profiles across declared contexts, one uniform violating root witness,
one uniform productive-safe root witness, and safe observational preservation
within each context. The root table binds a full interface and is recomputed.
A pointwise-valid family can still violate this assertion because its security
partition moves with context; the explicit division counterexample separates the
contracts. The inherited chance-accuracy quotient does not establish this result.

The full Cartesian baseline decides the finite expanded relation directly.
The certificate-free factored baseline uses the same decomposition and the same
number of root executions as the production checker. Thus the speed benefit is
factorization, not certificate packaging. We do not report a performance victory
over Cartesian Hoare Logic, Alive2, SMT-based noninterference, or general symbolic
execution: those systems were not run and have different supported semantics.

## Benchmark and empirical motivation

Risse and collaborators, Semantic Trap, PrimeVul, data-quality studies, and
SV-TrustEval-C motivate attention to pairing, context, and evaluation validity.
They are not executions repeated in this artifact. CodeXGLUE supplies a multi-task
benchmark context, not evidence that it measured the same shortcut failure as
those studies. SARD, Juliet, LAVA, Magma, vulnerability-fix collections, CVEfixes,
Big-Vul, and DiverseVul have their own ground-truth and provenance objectives;
none is described as deficient merely for not implementing this contract.

The present evidence is 36 owned contextual pairs, six root mechanisms and six
wrappers, with full small-product comparisons and classified controls. The
556-case compatibility audit concerns the sufficient fragment at zero nuisance
dimensions. It is not 556 independently transported native vulnerabilities.
Twenty-two anchored-valid but expanded-invalid controls show the missing
extrapolation obligation. Thirteen genuinely valid excluded controls show that
the proposed frame is conservative. Neither count estimates prevalence in an
external population.

## Stronger assurance work and unclaimed scope

Translation validation, verified validators, CompCert, kernel validation, Alive,
and Alive2 establish substantially richer compiler/language assurance. Proof-
carrying code, certifying algorithms, and correctness witnesses establish
consumer-checkable evidence. Native contract verification, WebSpec, and sound
protocol-verification pipelines supply semantic links absent from the owned IR.
The source capsule here is only an optional one-to-one syntax. No native-to-IR
refinement theorem or real-software deployment is implied.

Repair synthesis and overfitting studies separate candidate generation from
adequacy under a specification. Metamorphic testing and code perturbation work
already use controlled changes. The transport contract is not a claim to have
invented program perturbations. Delta debugging and reduction work likewise
preclude calling a fixed fourteen-feature search a general minimal causal
explanation.

## Contribution assessment

The current positive result is a specified, implemented, falsifiable finite
transport protocol with a soundness argument, a separating counterexample,
reusable checked interfaces, and measured incompleteness. Its practical reach is
not established by the owned corpus, and combining known verification principles
is not by itself evidence of a field-level methodological advance. A venue-level
significance judgment therefore cannot be inferred from passed tests, reference
count, or internal audit. The manuscript states the finite result and its limits
without claiming priority, production readiness, or an award-level outcome.
